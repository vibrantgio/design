Density

Two published settings share one variable family: :root carries comfortable, and a .compact class block overrides every per-setting metric of the --density-* family the way .dark overrides the colours — the right column below is the same markup inside a class="compact" wrapper. The dashed outline is --density-min-hit-target, the WCAG 2.5.5 pointer-target floor: it is not overridden, so compact shrinks the drawn control but never the clickable area.

Shadow depth

A level’s FILL is the platform’s own name for what that region is — the window’s plane, the chrome material, the content, the card. What the levels state here is the shadow each casts, the opt-in cue a floating transient carries on top of its fill: --shadow-N, for menus, dialogs and tooltips. Neither level under the content casts anything — the backdrop is what everything stands on, and chrome lies flat on it. The levels stop at 3 — desktop has no six-deep stack.
