Density

Two published settings share one variable family: :root carries comfortable, and a .compact class block overrides --density-control-height and --density-padding-x/-y the way .dark overrides the colours — the right column below is the same markup inside a class="compact" wrapper. The dashed outline is --density-min-hit-target, the WCAG 2.5.5 pointer-target floor: it is not overridden, so compact shrinks the drawn control but never the clickable area.

Elevation

Elevation is tonal (E2.1): a raised surface separates from its ground by colour, one neutral-ramp step per storey, and --elevation-N is that surface fill — the default cue, resolved as a var() reference into the neutral ramp so it flips with the mode. The cards below sit on the step-100 ground; level 0 is the bg pin, levels 1–3 are steps 200/300/400, and levels 4/5 clamp to level 3’s step — desktop has no six-storey stack.
