Buttons & tags

The button is .btn — filled by default, with .tonal and .ghost selecting the quieter emphasis registers. Emphasis is a colour property and nothing else: every register keeps the same size and the same focus ring. There is no size prop — heights come from --density-control-height, and density (.compact on any subtree) is the size axis. States shown statically wear the forcing twins (.is-hover, .is-active, .is-focus) that the token sheet groups into the live pseudo-class rules; disabled uses the native attribute.

Ghost — .btn.ghost

The quietest register: no ground at rest, neutral 700 text, and a neutral wash only under the pointer. For affordances that must be present without being the subject — a dialog's close X, a toolbar of icons. A ghost is quiet, not small: full height, full focus ring.

Tags — .tag

The chip the patterns draw: a Full-radius pill at label-small metrics, sized to its label, the line box plus one S1 tall. Filled (the accent pin under its on-colour) is the pricing card's "Popular" chip; .tag.tonal (primary 200 under the accent pin) is the hero's eyebrow, ringed in that pin because a tint and the pane it rests on are the same lightness and the pill's edge would not read otherwise. The pill itself has no interaction states; the dismissible chip's close affordance is a Gio interaction and has no class in this sheet.
