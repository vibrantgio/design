Forms

Form controls are native elements wearing the token sheet's classes — no script anywhere on this page. .input dresses a text <input>; a pop-up trigger is <select class="input select"> inside a .select-wrap, which draws the platform's pop-up mark; .checkbox and .radio dress their native input types. Resolution mirrors components/input and components/picker: the platform's text background under body-large text, the measured field edge (--platform-field-edge) on the resting edge of the field, the checkbox and the radio alike, the platform's placeholder text, focus adding the halo on the box the control already draws and moving nothing, and a switched-off control fading to the push button's own fill at --disabled-coverage over the surface it stands on. A text field's floor is --density-field-height and a trigger's is --density-control-height: the platform draws a field taller than the button beside it.

Menu — .menu

The surface a trigger opens: the window's own background under the measured floating shadow, which is the whole of what tells the menu's fill from the window's behind it, inside the platform's separator laid on the plane's own outline. A row is --density-control-height tall; the current one wears the pill and the check beside it. An open menu stands over its trigger with the current row on the trigger's own label rather than dropping below it, which is placement and the page's to arrange.

Checkbox — .checkbox

The measured 16 px glyph at every density, centred in --density-checkbox-row-height — the square footprint the platform gives a pointer, 22 px comfortable against the push button's 24 and the list row's 20. The glyph does not follow density and the footprint does. Its corner is the measured 5 px and its edge the measured 1 px of the platform's field hairline. Checked is the accent fill under a check mark in --platform-alternate-selected-control-text — the same figure the Gio side strokes on the icon set's 24-unit grid, drawn here out of two gradient bands. A fill says a colour was applied; only the mark says what that means. Switched off it is one fill and no edge at all.
