Forms

Form controls are native elements wearing the token sheet's classes — no script anywhere on this page. .input dresses a text <input>; a dropdown is <select class="input select"> inside a .select-wrap, which draws the chevron; .checkbox and .radio dress their native input types. Resolution mirrors components/input: Surface ground under body-large text, the rung the neutral ramp measures for a 3:1 graphic on the window ground (--color-control-border) on the resting edge of the field, the checkbox and the radio alike, neutral 700 placeholder, focus promoting the border to the accent pin, disabled fading every colour. Heights come from --density-control-height; density is the size axis.

Checkbox — .checkbox

A 20 px glyph at every density (the glyph does not shrink; the Gio side centres it in the control-height row and keeps the 44 px pointer target). Checked is the accent fill under a check mark in the on-accent pin — the same figure the Gio side strokes on the icon set's 24-unit grid, drawn here out of two gradient bands. A fill says a colour was applied; only the mark says what that means.
