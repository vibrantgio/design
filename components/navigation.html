Navigation

The four navigation patterns: .navbar, .tabs, .sidebar and .crumbs. All four rest their interactive cells on the surface ground, so hover is that ground's own one-rung walk to neutral-300 — the same wash the ghost button performs. Selection is the 2 px --color-accent underline (navbar links, tabs) or the two-step walk to primary-400 (sidebar rows). States shown statically wear the forcing twins (.is-hover, .is-focus) that the token sheet groups into the live pseudo-class rules.

Navbar — .navbar

A horizontal surface bar, --density-control-height plus twice --density-padding-y tall (52 px comfortable, 40 compact). Slots run brand, links, actions in document order; .navbar-links centres itself in the space the end slots leave over. Each .navbar-link is label-large text with a 2 px underline slot along its bottom edge; .selected fills the slot with the accent pin. Links clip rather than wrap — there is no overflow affordance.

Tabs — .tabs

A strip of .tab cells exactly --density-control-height tall over the surface ground. Each cell is its label plus --space-3 padding a side, with the same 2 px underline slot as a navbar link; .selected fills it with the accent pin. The content panel below the strip is yours — the strip carries no divider of its own.

Sidebar — .sidebar

A vertical surface rail at the pattern's two contractual widths: 192 px expanded, 48 px with .collapsed — component constants, deliberately not tokens. The .sidebar-toggle row and every .sidebar-item are exactly --density-control-height tall; each item leads with a 48 px .sidebar-item-icon column and its label starts at the column edge, one line, clipped — which is also what hides the labels at the collapsed width. .selected is the two-step walk to primary-400. The whole rail is one keyboard stop: the focus ring belongs to .sidebar itself, and the arrow keys move the selection.

Breadcrumb — .crumbs

A row of title-small segments with 12 px chevron separators (.crumb-sep) and --space-2 gaps. Colour follows position: the last .crumb is the current location at the text pin, ancestors rest on neutral-700 and hover to neutral-900 — the ghost register's text walk. .current forces the current-location colour on a specimen that is not last. Every segment renders on one line; a deep trail clips rather than collapsing to an ellipsis.

Compact density

The same markup inside a class="compact" wrapper: --density-control-height drops from 36 to 28 px, so the navbar bar, the tab strip and every sidebar row re-pitch. The sidebar's widths and the breadcrumb do not move — density is vertical rhythm, and a breadcrumb is a text row, not a control.

Dark mode

Nothing here is mode-specific: a class="dark" wrapper flips the colour variables — the surface grounds, the hover wash, the selected fill and the underlines all resolve from the paired ramp.
