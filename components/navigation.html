Navigation

The four navigation patterns — .navbar, .tabs, .sidebar and .crumbs — and .pane, the inset panel a sidebar stands in. All of them stand on the chrome, --platform-sidebar-material, and draw the separator where two flush regions meet. None of them tints under the pointer: the platform tints neither a list row nor a sidebar row, and that is the measured absence rather than an omission. What they draw is selection — the 2 px --platform-selected-content-background underline on a link or a tab, and the platform's own sidebar pill on a rail's row. States shown statically wear the forcing twins (.is-focus) that the token sheet groups into the live pseudo-class rules.

Navbar — .navbar

A horizontal chrome bar, --density-control-height plus twice --density-padding-y tall (28 px comfortable, 19 compact). Slots run brand, links, actions in document order; .navbar-links centres itself in the space the end slots leave over. Each .navbar-link is label-large text with a 2 px underline slot along its bottom edge; .selected fills the slot with the platform's selection colour. Links clip rather than wrap — there is no overflow affordance.

Tabs — .tabs

A strip of .tab cells exactly --density-control-height tall over the chrome. Each cell is its label plus --space-3 padding a side, with the same 2 px underline slot as a navbar link; .selected fills it with the platform's selection colour. The content panel below the strip is yours — the strip carries no seam of its own.

Sidebar — .sidebar inside .pane

The platform's sidebar is an inset rounded panel standing inside the window, not a flush column parted by a seam: .pane is that panel — 8 px off the window's edges, cornered at 18, wearing --platform-pane-rim just inside its edge and casting --platform-pane-shadow onto what stands beside it. The rim and the shadow are the boundary.

Inside it the rail draws its rows: a symbol, a label and, at the trailing end, a count when the entry has one, each part vertically centred, one line, clipped rather than wrapped — which is also what hides the label and the count at the collapsed width. Sections are headed by a small label in --platform-secondary-label and parted by that air alone; the platform draws no line there and neither does this. The row is 32 px, the symbol's 24 px box stands 17 in from the rail's leading edge, the label starts at 48 and the count ends 17 in from the trailing edge — every number measured. .selected wears the platform's sidebar pill, inset 10 from each edge and cornered at 8, and on it the label and the count both take the foreground the platform pairs with that fill. The two widths are the pattern's contractual 192 px expanded and 48 px with .collapsed, component constants and deliberately not tokens; collapsed, the symbol box is centred in the rail because there is no label to lead. The whole rail is one keyboard stop: the halo belongs to .sidebar itself, and the arrow keys move the selection.

Breadcrumb — .crumbs

A row of title-small segments with 12 px chevron separators (.crumb-sep) and --space-2 gaps. Colour follows position: the last .crumb is the current location in --platform-label, ancestors are links and take --platform-link. .current forces the current-location colour on a specimen that is not last. Every segment renders on one line; a deep trail clips rather than collapsing to an ellipsis.

Compact density

The same markup inside a class="compact" wrapper: --density-control-height drops from 24 to 19 px, so the navbar bar and the tab strip re-pitch. The sidebar's rows do not: a chrome rail's row is the pattern's own measured 32 px and not the density scale's. Neither do the sidebar's widths or the breadcrumb — a breadcrumb is a text row, not a control.

Dark mode

Nothing here is mode-specific: a class="dark" wrapper flips the colour variables — the chrome, the pill, the panel's rim and shadow and the underlines all resolve from the platform's dark set.
