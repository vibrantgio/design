Cards & elevation

The card is .card — a rounded surface container raised in place by tonal elevation: a raised surface separates from its ground by colour, one neutral-ramp step per storey, never by a cast shadow, because a card cannot float or leave. The default card fills at --elevation-1 under a 1 px neutral-500 strong stroke; .card.elevated trades the stroke for one storey deeper, --elevation-2. Geometry is --radius-lg corners, a --space-4 inset and --space-3 gaps between the stacked slots. A card styles no text of its own — header, body and footer are ordinary content whose typography you choose.

Outlined — .card

The default variant: the level-1 fill with the strong border. It sits one storey above the app's --color-bg ground.

Elevated — .card.elevated

One storey deeper: the level-2 fill, no stroke — and no shadow. Shadows are reserved for surfaces that float and can leave; a card is raised, not floating.

The elevation ladder

Each step is a surface fill: --elevation-0 is the bg pin, levels 1–3 are neutral steps 200/300/400, resolved as var() references into the ramp so they flip with dark mode. The ladder stops at 3 — desktop has no six-storey stack. The card lives at 1 and 2.

The opt-in shadow

The dp shadows survive as --shadow-N for floating transients only — menus, dialogs, toasts, tooltips: a float adds box-shadow: var(--shadow-N) on top of its tonal fill. Resting surfaces — and every card — use the fill alone.

Dark mode

Nothing here is mode-specific: a class="dark" wrapper flips the colour variables, and because --elevation-N is a reference into the neutral ramp, every storey flips with them.
